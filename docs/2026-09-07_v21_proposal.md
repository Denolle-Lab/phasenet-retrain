## 0. Why a proposal exists at all, and what must happen first

Task 3 found two things the recipe can address and one it cannot
(`docs/audit_2026-09-07/README.md`):

1. **A calibration offset on both phases** (H1). v7's probability at true
   arrivals sits 0.034 below the parent's for P and 0.070 for S at the
   median, with the two models ranking traces almost identically (Spearman
   0.87 for P, 0.97 for S). Rank-mapping v7 onto the parent's scale removes
   the whole deficit in every local bin, in regional P and in every SNR bin
   above 5 dB, leaving −0.009 [−0.016, −0.002] at regional S
   (`h1b_recalibration_residual`). This is the T = 4 distillation anchor
   (`scripts/fine_tune_model.py:264-275`) and the hard-argmax target
   (`scripts/fine_tune_model.py:238,262`): the student learns the parent's
   onsets with less confidence.
2. **A capability loss on low-SNR P that a threshold cannot remove** (H4).
   After recalibration v7 still loses 4.5 points of P recall below 0 dB and
   0.8 points at 0 to 5 dB, and the traces only the parent detects have a
   median SNR of 3.5 dB against 12.3 dB for the reverse set
   (`h4a`, `h4b`). Continuous data at 80 to 120 km, where most GeoNet and NOA
   picks lie, is this population. The corpus had no SNR curation
   (`scripts/build_training_dataset.py`, no SNR column is read) and the only
   noise augmentation tried was white noise (`scripts/fast_manifest_dataset.py:108-116`),
   which cost timing in v13 to v16.
3. **A uniform S under-confidence** (H2), not SNR-dependent: the S recall
   deficit is 3 to 6 points in every SNR bin and disappears under
   recalibration except at regional distance. Consistent with S being a
   minority of the supervision (23 % of the cap budget is P-only by policy,
   25 % of windows are teleseismic with S nulled). The per-bin S fraction of
   the manifest is still to be computed on the server.

What the recipe cannot address: v7 loses 10 points of teleseismic P recall
and the campaign is local and regional; this proposal drops teleseismic
supervision deliberately and accepts that loss.

The two external benchmarks also say that at matched pick budget the parent
still leads by 1 to 2 points on all six non-US sequence-phase pairs
(`external_recall_with_intervals`), so a recalibrated v7 would close most
but not all of the gap. A v21 is justified only if the low-SNR loss can be
reversed without the timing collapse of v13 to v16. The audit's closing
possibility stands: a parent trained on 6.1 million consistently labelled
waveforms may not be improvable by a half-million-window heterogeneous
fine-tune, and the twenty versions so far are consistent with that.

## 1. Data

- **Sources**: only datasets with analyst P **and** S picks. Drop the three
  P-only sets (`geofon`, `lendb`, `meier2019jgr`; `use_s=False` in
  `DATASET_CONFIGS`, `scripts/build_training_dataset.py:209-319`) and any
  source whose picks are automatic. STEAD and INSTANCE picks are largely
  automatic (`docs/2026-09-07_training_history_audit.md` §4.1); keep them
  only if their S picks are analyst-reviewed, which is to be checked in the
  metadata before the manifest is built, otherwise drop them too. The
  resulting corpus will be far smaller than 527k windows; that is
  acceptable, the parent is the prior.
- **Exclusions, in order** (all in `process_dataset`): benchmark traces and
  events, the held-out external sequences, the 2016 and 2021 whole-year
  hold-out, Aguilar-flagged label errors (`scripts/label_error_filter.py`,
  on by default).
- **No teleseismic rebalancing**: `TARGET_FRACTIONS` set to
  `{local 0.55, regional 0.45, teleseismic 0, unknown 0}` and the P-only
  teleseismic policy therefore inactive.
- **SNR stratification** (new): compute the pre-P/post-P RMS ratio at build
  time on the three components, store it in the manifest, and sample so that
  at least 35 % of windows are below 5 dB (the benchmark share is 33 %,
  `h4b`). Without this the low-SNR loss is not addressed.
- **Manifest verification**: `scripts/hash_manifests.py` extended with the
  new manifest family; `scripts/audit_heldout_sequences.py --check-manifest`
  must pass on train and val before training.

## 2. Augmentation (in `CachedManifestDataset.__getitem__`, all on the fly)

- **Random window position**: cut a longer window at cache time (the full
  trace where available) and choose the 3001-sample crop per draw so the P
  arrival falls uniformly between sample 300 and 2700, matching the
  benchmark's placement (`notebooks/05_benchmark_waveform_processing.ipynb`,
  `P_JITTER_MIN`/`P_JITTER_MAX`). This replaces the fixed 30 % placement in
  `scripts/manifest_dataset.py:227-247`. v7 showed no positional prior, v3
  did; this is hygiene, not the fix.
- **Real-noise superposition** from the existing pool
  (`data/noise_global`, `scripts/build_noise_dataset.py`), not Gaussian
  noise: with probability 0.5, add a noise trace scaled to a target SNR drawn
  uniformly from 0 to 25 dB, SNR defined on the P window as in the manifest
  column. Labels unchanged.
- **Band-limiting and resampling**: with probability 0.3, low-pass at a
  corner drawn from 8 to 20 Hz, then resample to a rate drawn from
  {40, 50, 80, 100} Hz and back to 100 Hz, so the model sees what the
  campaign's upsampled 40 Hz and 50 Hz instruments look like.
- **Channel dropout**: with probability 0.1 zero one horizontal component;
  with probability 0.05 zero both horizontals (S then unlabelled? no: keep
  the S label, the parent was trained with full three-component data and the
  campaign meets single-component stations).
- Keep the existing amplitude jitter and polarity flip.

## 3. Loss

- **Soft Gaussian targets**: cross-entropy against the σ = 10-sample
  Gaussian label as the parent was trained (`soft_ce: true`,
  `scripts/fine_tune_model.py:238-245`). v20 tested this alone on the old
  data and lost; here it is combined with the data and augmentation changes,
  and its effect is to be isolated with the ablation in §5.
- **Distillation**: start at α = 0.3 with T = 1.5 (v11's setting, the best
  detector among the fine-tunes on the noise pool) and, as a second arm,
  α = 0. No timing, presence, focal or class-weight terms.
- LR 5e-6 AdamW as before; early stopping on validation loss with the
  held-out split never read (§4).

## 4. Selection rule

- Three splits from the new manifest: train, val (early stopping only), and
  a **selection split** that no script reads until the run is declared
  finished. The selection split is built from whole datasets and whole
  years, not traces, and its composition is written to
  `data/manifest_checksums.csv` before training starts.
- On the selection split, the pre-registered metrics are, in this order:
  recall at matched pick budget against the parent on the noise-pool plus
  positives (`scripts/compute_detection_metrics.py` extended to report the
  budget curve), conditional P and S MAE on the traces both detect, and the
  detection MCC at each model's own best threshold. Bootstrap intervals from
  `scripts/metrics.py::bootstrap_ci` on everything. The benchmark of
  `notebooks/step3_metrics.csv` is not read for selection.
- One candidate checkpoint per arm goes to the acceptance test.

## 5. Arms and budget

| Arm | Change from v7 | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A | §1 data + §2 augmentation + soft CE, α = 0.3, T = 1.5 | the proposal |
| B | A with α = 0 | is the anchor still needed once the data no longer damage the parent |
| C | A without real-noise superposition and without SNR stratification | isolates the low-SNR intervention, the one that matters |

Three runs, each about the length of v7 (44 epochs on 527k windows took the
server one night; the corpus here is smaller). If arm C matches arm A at
matched budget, the low-SNR intervention did nothing and the parent stands.
