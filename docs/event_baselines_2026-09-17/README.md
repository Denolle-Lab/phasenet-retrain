# Event-level baselines on the regression and development cases (36B, provisional)

*2026-09-17, branch `issue/36b-event-baselines` from
`audit/2026-09-07-generalization` (b5fd078). Checkpoint 36B of issue #36,
run on the laptop CPU with pyocto 0.2.0, SeisBench 0.12.5, PyTorch 2.2.2,
Python 3.11. Provisional because the picks are at the 35B matched-budget
operating points, not the 35C calibrated ones, and the associator parameters
are the 36A proposals, untuned. Every number in this file comes from the
committed CSVs of this directory; the per-run artifacts are under
`data/evaluation/events/<key>/<weight>/` (gitignored, regenerated by the
command at the end).*

## What was run

The three PhaseNet weights of 35B (`docs/baselines_2026-09-13/README.md`:
`jma_wc` the parent, `instance` the second parent candidate, `quakescope2026`
the deployed export of `jma_wc_ft_global_v7`) were scored again with
`scripts/heldout_testset_score.py` on the seven cases whose role is
regression or development (`configs/evaluation_suites.json`; no acceptance
case was touched, the 44A guard refuses them). The aggregate rows reproduce
`docs/baselines_2026-09-13/matched_budget.csv` cell for cell (checked with
`budget.parquet` of every run: 0 differing cells). The pick stores of those
runs (`access_id` per case in `runs.csv`) are the input of every association.

Each pick store was associated once per weight with
`scripts/event_association.py --associator pyocto --sequence <key>`, which
takes the regime config from `heldout_testset_registry.py`
(`configs/association/msas.json` be32d8ffcaa3 for Kaikōura, Norcia,
Thessaly, Adriatic and Samos, whose registry regime is `msas`;
`vt.json` 703aa7c593a1 for Etna; `swarm.json` b9ed1db8b94c for
Corinth–Thiva), the station table, the catalogue, the windows and the
mainshock time. 21 runs, 52.8 s of PyOcto time in total, 64.3 s of wall
time including matching and tables (`runs.csv`, `associate_runtime_s`,
`wall_s`). No run returned zero events, so no failure row was needed.

Matching: one-to-one maximum-cardinality assignment with the 36A tolerances,
origin time 5 s, epicentre 30 km, depth 50 km. Denominator: catalogue events
whose origin lies inside a scoring window (Etna and Corinth–Thiva carry the
whole 2022–2024 and 2020–2021 catalogues, 2068 and 1016 events, of which 108
and 21 are in the two 3 h windows).

## Reading

- **`instance` recovers more catalogue events than the parent at the
  parent's pick budget.** Pooled over the four certified mainshock cases the
  difference is +0.063 [+0.028, +0.097] on 431 events; over all six
  eligible cases +0.066 [+0.038, +0.095] on 560. Per case the interval
  excludes zero on Kaikōura (+0.153), Adriatic (+0.086) and Etna (+0.074),
  not on Norcia (+0.013), Thessaly (−0.027, upper end exactly 0) or
  Corinth–Thiva (+0.095 on 21 events). This is the event-level version of
  the 35B pick-level result, where `instance` led on S on every case; the
  lead carries through the associator on the cases where the parent misses
  events, and disappears where the parent already recovers above 0.9
  (Thessaly). Norcia, Adriatic and Etna are Italian and the INSTANCE
  training exposure caveat of 35B applies; Kaikōura carries no such caveat.
- **The v7 export is indistinguishable from its parent at event level.**
  Every Δ interval contains zero; the pooled point estimates are −0.016
  (mainshock) and −0.013 (all eligible); on Corinth–Thiva v7 matches
  exactly the parent's 19 events (degenerate interval), on Etna the same
  count (95) with two events swapped. Same conclusion as 35B and as the
  2026-09-07 audit.
- **Kaikōura is the regression case and the lowest recovery (0.48 for the
  parent).** Half of that is station support, not the picker: only 67 of the
  137 catalogue events have GeoNet P picks on three of the six scored
  stations (37A `events_3sta_P_fetched`), and among those the parent
  recovers 0.64, `instance` 0.79, v7 0.54. Of the parent's 71 unmatched
  covered events, 11 have a predicted event within 5 s but more than 30 km
  away and 33 have one 5–30 s away (`unmatched_reference.csv`,
  `nearest_class`): a six-station network at 50–200 km with a homogeneous
  6.0/3.4 km/s model locates poorly, and the 30 km tolerance then fails the
  match. The catalogue's magnitude floor in the window is 3.4 (37A
  `mag_min`) against a 2.5 query minimum: GeoNet's first three hours after
  the M7.8 are incomplete below M3.4, which is also why 105 of the parent's
  171 predicted events are unmatched.
- **Norcia is the only case with merges** (4 for every weight, `merges`
  column; `data/evaluation/events/norcia_2016/<weight>/diagnostics.json`
  lists the reference pairs, 3–5 picks from each): 150 catalogue events in
  2 h, so pairs of catalogue events within 5 s and 30 km of one predicted
  event occur. `instance` and v7 each also produce one split. The `within
  tolerances` rows of `unmatched_reference.csv` (4 for the parent, 3 for
  `instance`, 4 for v7) are the merge partners whose feasible predicted
  event went to the other catalogue event.
- **Magnitude** (`recovery_by_magnitude.csv`): no case shows a clean
  fall-off toward its lowest populated bin. Adriatic [1, 2) (16 events):
  parent 0.81, `instance` 0.94, v7 0.88 against 0.91, 0.98, 0.94 in
  [2, 3) and 0.62–0.75 in [3, 4) (8 events); Etna [1, 2) (32): 0.78, 0.94,
  0.75 against 0.91–0.96 in [2, 3); Norcia [2, 3) (43) 0.77–0.81 and
  [3, 4) (97) 0.79–0.85. Kaikōura is flat for the parent and v7 across
  [3, 4), [4, 5) and M≥5 (0.42–0.56; 16, 95 and 26 events) while
  `instance` reaches 0.77 on M≥5.
- **Hour after mainshock** (`recovery_by_hour.csv`): every row of the five
  mainshock cases has `claim_supported = False`, because the windows cover
  0.833 of the [0, 1) h bin (they start 600 s after the mainshock) and
  0.233 (Norcia) or 0.433 of the [1, 6) h bin. The values are reported for
  reading only: parent [0, 1) h 0.40 (Kaikōura), 0.78 (Norcia), 1.00
  (Thessaly), 0.81 (Adriatic); [1, 6) h 0.52, 0.85, 0.93, 0.89. On Etna and
  Corinth–Thiva there is no mainshock, `time_origin_label` is
  `first_window_start` and the hour rows count hours after the first busy
  window, not after any event; their [0, 1) h rows are supported
  (coverage 1.0) but describe the first hour of a busy-window excerpt. No
  first-48-hour or migration claim is possible from these windows.
- **Day of swarm** (`recovery_by_day.csv`, days with covered events only):
  Etna's two windows fall on days 0 and 278 after the first window start,
  Corinth–Thiva's on days 0 and 61; each day row has `coverage_fraction`
  0.125 (3 h of 24) and `claim_supported = False`. Etna day 0 (65 events):
  parent 0.85, `instance` 0.94, v7 0.82; day 278 (43): 0.93, 0.98, 0.98.
  Corinth–Thiva day 0 (10): 0.90, 1.00, 0.90; day 61 (11): 0.91, 1.00, 0.91.
- **Station support** (`station_support.csv`, predicted events by number of
  stations): every case has six scored stations with no common-support
  exclusion and no annotation failure (`excluded.parquet`,
  `failures.parquet` empty in all seven scoring runs). Matched events sit at
  5–6 stations; the unmatched ones at 3–4 (Corinth–Thiva parent: 37 of 84
  predicted events on 3 stations, none matched; Kaikōura parent: 65 on 4
  stations, 19 matched). Median stations per predicted event 5 (mainshock
  cases), 6 (Adriatic, Etna), 4 (Corinth–Thiva). Valid exposure per station
  (the 35A valid mask) is not joined here; the pick store carries picks,
  not the per-sample mask, and 37A reports 0.998–1.0 three-component
  coverage on every case in windows.
- **Unassociated picks**: 31–83 % of the selected picks enter no event
  (Norcia `instance` 738 of 2362 at the low end, Samos parent 946 of 1142
  at the high end, Corinth–Thiva parent 1619 of 2154). With six stations and `min_picks` 5–6 plus two
  stations with both phases, an event needs most of the network; single
  picks, picks of events outside the region box and picks of events below
  the minimum are left over. The count is a property of the operating point
  and the configuration together and is not a false-pick rate.
- **Unmatched predicted events** (`unmatched_predicted.csv`, one row each
  with the nearest catalogue event, its time and distance offset, and
  `nearest_class`). For the parent: Kaikōura 105 (11 within 5 s of a
  catalogue event but beyond 30 km, 31 at 5–30 s, 60 at 30–120 s, 3
  further); Norcia 67 (5, 37, 25, 0); Thessaly 131 (0, 16, 95, 20);
  Adriatic 39 (0, 9, 25, 5); Etna 75 (0, 11, 48, 16); Corinth–Thiva 65 (0,
  3, 16, 46); Samos 25 (0, 0, 4, 21). Catalogue absence is not proof of a
  false event: the catalogues are magnitude-truncated at the query minimum
  (2.5 Kaikōura, 2.0 Norcia, Thessaly, Samos, 1.5 Adriatic, 1.0 Etna and
  Corinth–Thiva catalogue queries, `heldout_testset_registry.py`) and in
  the windows their floors are 3.4, 2.4, 2.1, 3.0, 1.5, 1.4, 1.2 (37A
  `mag_min`), while six stations at tens of km detect smaller events. The
  rows are the sample for the independent review the 36B acceptance asks
  for; that review has not been done and none of these events is called
  false or real here.

## Caveats that keep this provisional

1. **35B thresholds, not 35C.** Each weight sits at the threshold where it
   emits the parent's pick count at the parent's 0.3. The calibrated
   nuisance-budget operating points of 35C do not exist yet; the numbers
   here compare weights at equal pick volume, not at equal false-pick rate.
2. **Associator parameters are the 36A proposals, untuned**, homogeneous
   velocities per regime, the same file for every weight (hash in
   `runs.csv`). Nothing was changed after seeing results. PyOcto prints for
   every run that it raises `n_s_picks` from the configured 0 to
   `n_p_and_s_picks` (2) internally; the configuration file and its hash
   are unchanged by that. Kaikōura's location scatter (above) is the case
   that would benefit first from a regional 1-D model; that is a 36B
   follow-up on development cases only, never on acceptance.
3. **Short windows.** One 2–3 h window per mainshock case starting 600 s
   after the mainshock, two 3 h busy windows for Etna and Corinth–Thiva.
   `claim_supported` is False on every hour and day row of the mainshock
   cases and on every day row overall; the pooled interval is over 431 or
   560 events from at most 13 h of data.
4. **Catalogue completeness** is set by the query minimum and by the
   agencies' early-aftershock processing (Kaikōura floor 3.4 in the
   window). Recovery is relative to those catalogues; the unmatched
   predicted events are unreviewed.
5. **Samos and Corinth–Thiva eligibility (37A).** Samos supports neither
   pick nor event scoring (6 events with 3-station support, 3 covered
   stations with references); its rows are listed and excluded from every
   pooled row. Corinth–Thiva supports event scoring (20 events) but not S
   pick scoring; it is in the all-eligible pool, not the mainshock pool.
   The registry places Samos in the `msas` regime and it was associated
   with `msas.json`.
6. **34C not released**, as in 35B: the annotations are SeisBench
   `annotate` output at each weight's own preprocessing; parity with the
   training loader is unverified.
7. **Six stations per case.** Intervals on 16–150 events are wide; the
   `instance` lead is established pooled and on three cases, directional
   elsewhere.

## Validation

- `python -m pytest tests -q` in the venv: 407 passed, 32 subtests passed.
  `tests/test_event_association.py` holds 46 tests, 4 new: the real PyOcto
  adapter on the synthetic 4-event fixture with 20 noise picks (4 of 4
  recovered, |dt| < 1 s, < 5 km, no noise pick assigned, PyOcto's
  `n_picks`, `n_p_picks`, `n_p_and_s_picks`, `pick_match_tolerance`,
  `min_node_size`, `time_before` and `zlim` equal to the config's; skipped
  when pyocto is absent), per-phase pick selection, the recorded per-phase
  operating point in `run.json`, and the CLI flags.
- pyocto 0.2.0 API: `OctoAssociator.from_area(lat, lon, zlim,
  velocity_model, time_before, **kwargs)`, `VelocityModel0D(p_velocity,
  s_velocity, tolerance, association_cutoff_distance)` and the rest match
  the 36A adapter's calls; the one change needed was in the result
  mapping, because 0.2's `associate` returns the assignments already joined
  with the pick columns (the 0.1-style merge on `pick_idx` collided on
  `time` and `station`). The adapter now takes every pick attribute from
  its own frame by `pick_idx`.
- The scoring regeneration reproduces `docs/baselines_2026-09-13/matched_budget.csv`
  exactly (5:52 wall for annotation and the dense-grid extraction, three
  weights, seven cases). The association driver was run three times on
  the same stores (before and after the two commits of this branch);
  every count in `recovery_by_case.csv` was identical each time.
